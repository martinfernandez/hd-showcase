Before you start

  1. Open the deck on the projector laptop. The chip at the bottom right must say LIVE; DEMO means ?demo is in the URL.
  2. Press C and click Reset all. The deck jumps to the title and every phone goes back to "Join the game".
  3. Press F for fullscreen. If you share the screen, click once inside the deck so the keys reach it.
  4. Scan the QR with your own phone to check it loads on the room's wifi.
